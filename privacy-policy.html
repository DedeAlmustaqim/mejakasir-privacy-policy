Privacy Policy – Meja Kasir

Effective Date: June 4, 2026

1. Introduction

Meja Kasir is a fully offline point-of-sale (POS) application designed to help users manage sales, products, reports, and business operations directly on their device.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Meja Kasir handles data, device permissions, security features, and in-app purchases.

Meja Kasir does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal or sensitive user data.

2. Data Collection and Storage

All application data, including transactions, products, reports, and settings, is stored locally on the user's device.

Meja Kasir does not require account registration or login and does not transmit user data to external servers.

3. Permissions Used

To support application functionality, Meja Kasir may request the following Android permissions:

Storage Access

  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: Used to import backup files created by the user.
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: Used on Android 9 and below to export backup data to local device storage.
  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / Document Access: Used on Android 13+ to access product images and backup files selected by the user.

Bluetooth Access

  • BLUETOOTH / BLUETOOTH_CONNECT / BLUETOOTH_SCAN: Used to connect and print to compatible Bluetooth thermal printers.

Location Access

  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION: May be required by the Android operating system on Android 10–11 to enable Bluetooth device scanning. Meja Kasir does not collect, track, or store location information.

Biometric and Device Authentication

  • USE_BIOMETRIC: Used to allow device authentication for protecting access to sensitive sections of the application.
  • USE_FINGERPRINT: May be used where applicable on older Android versions.

Meja Kasir may use the device's built-in authentication mechanisms, such as fingerprint, facial recognition, PIN, password, or screen lock pattern, to help protect sensitive areas of the application.

Important: Meja Kasir does not collect, store, process, or transmit biometric information. Biometric verification is performed entirely by the Android operating system. The application only receives a confirmation result indicating whether authentication was successful or unsuccessful.

All permissions are used locally on the device and only when the corresponding feature is actively used by the user.

4. Backup and Restore

Users may manually create and restore backups using local device storage.

Backup files remain under the user's control and are never uploaded to external servers by Meja Kasir.

5. In-App Purchases

Meja Kasir offers a one-time Premium upgrade through Google Play Billing.

All purchase transactions are securely processed by Google Play. Meja Kasir does not collect, store, or access payment card information, banking details, or other financial data.

6. Children's Privacy

Meja Kasir is not intended for children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Data Security

Because Meja Kasir operates fully offline, users retain full control over their data.

Security features such as device authentication, including fingerprint, face recognition, PIN, password, or screen lock pattern, may be used to help protect sensitive areas of the application.

Users are encouraged to create regular backups of their data using the built-in backup feature.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Any future updates will be published within the application, on the Google Play Store listing, or on the associated privacy policy page.

9. Contact

If you have questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us using the email address provided on the Google Play Store listing.
